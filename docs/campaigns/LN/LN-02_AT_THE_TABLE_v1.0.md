# LN-02 — За столом

**Campaign:** LN — «Остання ніч»  
**Mission role:** parallel-text investigation / evidence synthesis

## 1. Mission purpose

This mission moves the player from locating the supper room (LN-01) to examining what the Gospel witnesses explicitly report at the table before the betrayal investigation becomes the focus of LN-03.

The learning goal is not to test denominational doctrine about the Eucharist/Lord's Supper. The player instead learns to reconstruct the shared narrative actions and wording patterns in Matthew, Mark and Luke, identify details that belong to only one witness, notice where translation/manuscript variation affects wording, and support conclusions with passage evidence.

The mission deliberately avoids forcing a single doctrinal interpretation of phrases such as «this is my body» or «blood of the covenant». Those questions belong to later interpretive content and must be labelled I1/D1 where appropriate.

## 2. Primary Scripture corpus

Primary:

- Matthew 26:20, 26–29
- Mark 14:17, 22–25
- Luke 22:14–20

Secondary optional cross-reference:

- 1 Corinthians 11:23–26

The betrayal-identification material in Matthew 26:21–25, Mark 14:18–21, Luke 22:21–23 and John 13 is reserved primarily for LN-03 so that LN-02 retains a clear learning focus.

## 3. Source-audit findings

Source audit on 2026-08-17 confirmed the following direct-text facts:

- Matthew and Mark place Jesus at the evening meal with the Twelve and then narrate the bread and cup while they are eating.
- Luke says that when the hour came Jesus reclined at table with the apostles and explicitly states that he had strongly/eagerly desired to eat this Passover with them before suffering.
- Matthew, Mark and Luke all contain a bread action in which Jesus takes bread, gives thanks/blessing, breaks it and gives it; all connect the bread with his body.
- Matthew and Mark both narrate a cup given after thanksgiving and connect it with covenant blood poured out/shed for many.
- Luke 22:20 connects the cup with the new covenant in Jesus' blood and says it is poured out for the recipients; Luke also includes an earlier cup in 22:17–18 before the bread.
- Matthew explicitly includes «for forgiveness of sins» in connection with the covenant blood; Mark does not include that phrase in its parallel sentence.
- Matthew, Mark and Luke all include a forward-looking saying about not drinking again until the kingdom-related future.
- Luke additionally says, in the bread saying, «do this in remembrance of me» in the common longer textual form represented by many modern translations.
- 1 Corinthians 11:23–26 independently preserves a bread/cup tradition and explicitly includes remembrance/proclamation language.

### Textual-transmission cautions

The mission must not pretend that every translation/manuscript presents every word identically.

- Luke 22:19b–20 has a significant textual variant: some manuscripts omit the longer wording beginning with the giving of the body and the cup saying. Modern translations commonly disclose this in a footnote.
- Matthew 26:28 and Mark 14:24 have manuscript variation affecting whether «new» appears before «covenant» in some textual traditions/translations.
- Translation wording varies for blessing/giving thanks, cup wording, poured out/shed, and kingdom phrasing.

Therefore no player is penalized for a translation-equivalent wording, and no task treats a known textual variant as if all manuscripts were identical.

No historical/contextual claim outside the defined Scripture corpus is required for mission completion.

## 4. Learning objectives

By the end of LN-02, the player should be able to:

1. identify the principal Gospel passages for the table/bread/cup episode;
2. reconstruct the shared action sequence around bread and cup without importing later tradition into the text;
3. identify Luke-only details within the defined corpus, especially the explicit desire to eat the Passover before suffering and the additional cup before the bread;
4. identify Matthew's explicit «for forgiveness of sins» wording without falsely attributing it to Mark;
5. distinguish a shared Gospel core from witness-specific wording;
6. recognize that «not present in this witness» is different from «contradicted by this witness»;
7. handle translation and textual-transmission variation without false certainty;
8. cite Scripture as evidence for a reconstruction;
9. retrieve the LN-01 discipline of separating direct text from inference.

## 9. Opening brief

**Case file:** The room has been found and the meal begins. Three Gospel witnesses preserve overlapping material at the table, but their wording and sequencing are not identical.

**Case question:** What can be established directly about the meal, the bread, the cup and the forward-looking sayings — and which details belong to a particular witness rather than to all three?

**Investigation rule:** A detail missing from one account is not automatically a contradiction. State only what the evidence permits.

## 10. Known facts at start

- The Passover meal has been prepared (from LN-01).
- Matthew, Mark and Luke all preserve material about the meal.

## 11. Unknowns to resolve

- Which details are shared by all three witnesses?
- Which detail is explicit in Luke but not in Matthew/Mark in the defined corpus?
- Which wording is explicit in Matthew but not Mark?
- How should the player deal with Luke's additional cup and textual variants?
- What is the safest evidence-based reconstruction of the bread/cup sequence without forcing a harmonization beyond the text?
