2. Відновити локальну послідовність Марка 14:55–65.
3. Виявити, що обидва синоптики прямо говорять про неправдиві свідчення та їхню недостатність/неузгодженість.

5. Розрізнити мовчання Ісуса перед свідченнями та Його відповідь на питання первосвященника.
6. Порівняти питання «Месія/Христос» і «Син Божий/Син Благословенного» без примусової уніфікації формулювань.

8. Виявити спільний мотив Сина Людського праворуч Сили/Бога та небесної перспективи.
9. Відрізнити те, що Матвій/Марко описують як нічний обвинувальний блок, від Лукиного чітко позначеного денного зібрання.

### Primary Scripture

### Contrast-only Scripture

### Source-audit summary

**Matthew 26:59–60:** chief priests and whole council seek false testimony to put Jesus to death; many false witnesses come, but sufficient testimony is not obtained.  
**Matthew 26:60–61:** two finally come forward with a temple-related accusation.  
**Matthew 26:62–63:** high priest asks for an answer; Jesus remains silent; then the high priest puts the Messiah/Son of God question under oath.  
**Matthew 26:64:** Jesus answers with Matthew’s wording and adds the Son of Man/right-hand/clouds declaration.  
**Matthew 26:65–66:** high priest tears garments, labels the statement blasphemy, asks for judgment, and the response is that Jesus is worthy of death.  
**Matthew 26:67–68:** spitting, striking/slapping and the “prophesy” taunt follow.

**Mark 14:55–56:** chief priests and whole council seek testimony to put Jesus to death; many give false testimony, but their testimonies do not agree.  
**Mark 14:57–59:** some give a temple-related false testimony, but even this testimony does not agree.  
**Mark 14:60–61:** high priest questions Jesus about testimony; Jesus remains silent; then asks whether He is the Messiah, Son of the Blessed One.  
**Mark 14:62:** Mark directly records “I am” in common English translations, followed by Son of Man/right-hand/clouds language.  
**Mark 14:63–64:** high priest tears garments, says no more witnesses are needed, invokes blasphemy, and all condemn Him as deserving death.  
**Mark 14:65:** spitting, face-covering, striking and “Prophesy” follow.

**Luke 22:66:** explicitly begins when day came/at daybreak, with elders, chief priests and scribes gathered and Jesus led before the council.  
**Luke 22:67–69:** question whether He is the Christ/Messiah; Jesus replies that they would not believe/answer, then speaks of the Son of Man seated at the right hand of the power of God.  
**Luke 22:70:** all ask whether He is then the Son of God; Jesus responds with Luke’s own wording.  
**Luke 22:71:** they conclude that they have heard what they need from His own mouth.

### Disputed / non-forced points

- The mission does **not** grade one exact cross-Gospel minute-by-minute order of all hearings.
- It does **not** equate John 18:19–24 with Matthew/Mark’s false-witness sequence.
- It does **not** force one English rendering of Jesus’ reply in Matthew/Luke where translations differ in idiom.
- It does **not** require the player to decide a full historical/legal reconstruction beyond what the cited texts directly establish.

## 6. Narrative shell

### Opening brief

У LN-07 ви простежили маршрут після арешту й навчилися тримати окремо локальні послідовності свідків. Тепер перед вами інший тип проблеми: не «куди повели Ісуса?», а **які свідчення висували, що в них не сходилося, яке питання стало вирішальним і що саме кожен євангеліст прямо стверджує**.

### Case question

Який доказовий шлях веде від суперечливих/неприйнятих свідчень до формулювання звинувачення й рішення, якщо ми не змішуємо Матвія, Марка і Луку в один текст?

### Known facts at start

- LN-07 already established a post-arrest route and the Luke daybreak boundary.
- John’s questioning scene is provenance-distinct from the Matthew/Mark witness scene.
- Peter-denial fulfilment is not investigated here.

### Unknowns to resolve

- Що саме Матвій і Марко називають проблемою свідчень?
- Як відрізняються храмові формулювання?

- Що Лука переносить у чітко денну сцену?
- Яку частину можна назвати спільним ядром, а яку — тільки деталлю конкретного свідка?

# 10. Branching and recovery

### Evidence-order branch

After N01 the player may inspect Matthew first or Mark first. Both routes converge at N04. The chosen order changes only information order, not truth conditions.

### Correction branch

If N04 merges the two temple sayings, the game unlocks a forced comparison screen/text sequence:
1. Matthew 26:61 only;
2. Mark 14:58 only;
3. identify shared core;
4. identify Mark-only wording;
5. retry N04 with guided mastery flag.

### Chronology correction

If N10 is answered as “night,” the mission replays LN-07’s Luke 22:66 daybreak anchor before allowing N11.

### Provenance correction

If N13 imports false witnesses into Luke, O01 may unlock early to demonstrate that parallel witness blocks have distinct content.

### Hint threshold

At hint level 6 or 7 the decisive verse reference/answer is exposed; mission completion remains possible, but the affected mastery evidence is `guided` and scheduled for later retrieval.

# 11. Completion synthesis

A successful player should leave LN-08 with this evidence-aware model:

1. Matthew and Mark directly describe an effort to obtain testimony against Jesus and explicitly characterize the testimony as false/problematic.
2. Mark explicitly emphasizes non-agreement of testimony, including the temple-related testimony; Matthew identifies two who finally present a temple-related accusation.
3. Jesus is silent before the witness-questioning stage, then the scene turns to the direct Messiah/Son question.
4. Matthew and Mark preserve related but non-identical wording for both the question and Jesus’ reply.
5. Both connect the answer with the Son of Man/right-hand/cloud imagery.
6. Luke’s cited council scene begins explicitly at daybreak/when day came and structures the questioning differently.
7. Luke 22:66–71 must not be made to say that it narrates Matthew/Mark’s false-witness or temple-accusation details.
8. John 18:19–24 remains a distinct provenance contrast, not a hidden fourth version of the same witness procedure.
