**Date:** 2026-08-19

## Rule
`TX1` is an adjunct textual-variant flag. It never replaces `confidence_code`, never requires the player to decide originality, and never makes a responsible translation/text form an error merely for representing an attested variant differently.

## Named trace records

### Luke 22:19b–20
- Named basis: **NET Bible, 2nd ed., textual-critical (`tc`) note at Luke 22:19–20**.
- Supports: recognition that the longer wording in this unit has significant manuscript/transmission variation, including witnesses representing a shorter form.
- Gameplay effect: disclose TX1 before affected grading; accept responsible longer/shorter/bracketed/footnoted forms at the proposition level.
- Does **not** support: forcing the player to declare which form is original or deriving a doctrinal verdict from the existence of the variant.

### Matthew 26:28 / Mark 14:24 covenant wording
- Named basis: **NET Bible textual-critical notes attached to the covenant wording in Matthew 26:28 and Mark 14:24**, used together with responsible modern translation footnotes.

- Gameplay effect: preserve witness attribution and semantic equivalence; do not require the word `new` as a universal Matthew/Mark string.
- Does **not** support: originality adjudication as a required answer.
