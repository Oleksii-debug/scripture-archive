### Source audit notes
- Matthew 26:69–75 presents Peter in the courtyard. A servant girl associates him with Jesus the Galilean; Peter denies before all. At the gateway another servant girl says he was with Jesus of Nazareth; Peter denies with an oath that he does not know the man. Later bystanders identify him as one of the group, with his speech/accent as evidence. Peter invokes curses and swears that he does not know the man. A rooster crows; Peter remembers Jesus’ prediction and goes out and weeps bitterly.
- Mark 14:66–72 presents a servant girl of the high priest seeing Peter warming himself. She associates him with Jesus the Nazarene; he denies knowledge/understanding and moves toward the entryway. The same servant girl speaks again to those present that he is one of them; Peter denies again. Later bystanders identify him as one of them because he is a Galilean. Peter curses and swears that he does not know the man. In the widespread textual form the rooster crows a second time; Peter remembers the prediction that before the rooster crows twice he will deny Jesus three times. Some early manuscript traditions omit one or both second-crow references; this is `TX1` and may not be used to penalize responsible translations.
- Luke 22:54–62 places Peter following at a distance and sitting at a courtyard fire. A servant girl identifies him as having been with Jesus; Peter says he does not know him. A little later another person says he is one of them; Peter denies it. About an hour later another insists that Peter was with Jesus because he is a Galilean. Peter denies knowing what the accuser means; immediately the rooster crows. Luke uniquely states that the Lord turned and looked at Peter; Peter remembers the prediction, goes out, and weeps bitterly.
- John 18:15–18,25–27 interleaves Peter’s denial material with the high-priestly questioning. Another disciple, known to the high priest, gains Peter entry. The servant girl keeping the door asks whether Peter is one of Jesus’ disciples; he says he is not. Peter stands warming himself by a charcoal fire with servants and officers. Later they again ask whether he is one of Jesus’ disciples; he denies. A servant of the high priest who is a relative of the man whose ear Peter cut off asks whether he saw Peter in the garden with Jesus. Peter denies again; immediately a rooster crows.
- All four witnesses support three denial episodes followed by a rooster marker, but they do not give an identical roster of accusers, wording, spatial movement, elapsed time, or post-rooster details.
- Matthew and Luke explicitly narrate Peter going out and weeping bitterly; John 18:15–18,25–27 does not state this. Mark narrates Peter’s emotional reaction in wording that varies by translation; the mission must not force one English/Ukrainian rendering as the only acceptable semantic form.

### Disputed / limited-certainty points
- Exact one-to-one identification of every accuser across all four Gospels is not warranted by the passages (`D1`).
- Exact harmonized micro-order of all courtyard movements, high-priestly questioning, each accusation, and each rooster event is not forced (`D1`).
- Mark 14:68,72 has material textual variation involving the first crow and «second time/twice» language (`TX1`). A responsible translation/manuscript tradition that lacks or footnotes those elements is accepted.
- The identity of John’s «other disciple» in 18:15–16 is not stated by personal name in the assigned text and must not be graded as a direct T1 identification.

## 7. Completion synthesis

Кампанійний доказ із LN-04 тепер пройшов перевірку виконанням. Чотири Євангелія узгоджуються щодо ядра: Петро тричі заперечує свою належність/знання Ісуса, і півнячий маркер завершує цю послідовність. Але сила гри полягає в тому, щоб не стерти відмінності. Матвій зберігає акцент/мовлення, клятви й гіркий плач; Марко — власний склад обвинувачів і текстологічно варіантний другий спів; Лука — часовий проміжок, погляд Господа, пам’ять і плач; Іван — привратницю, вугільний вогонь і родича Малха, який апелює до саду. Спільне ядро можна впевнено синтезувати; точну тотожність усіх обвинувачів і одну мікрохронологію чотирьох текстів — ні.

## 8. Reusable design result established in LN-09

### Prediction-to-fulfilment delta matrix

Future prediction/fulfilment missions should use this sequence:
1. retrieve the prediction before showing fulfilment;
2. lock prediction anchors and confidence;
3. collect fulfilment witnesses independently;
4. confirm the narrow shared fulfilment core;
5. record witness-specific deltas rather than flatten them;
6. expose textual variants before grading where they affect matching;
7. state the limit of harmonized certainty;
8. schedule weak prediction/fulfilment dimensions for later spaced retrieval.

This pattern extends LN-04’s prediction-to-fulfilment retrieval rule into a complete two-stage mechanic.

### Denial-count safeguard

The game counts **three denial episodes**, not «three identical sentences» and not «three universally identical accusers». Different witnesses may frame the episodes with different speakers, wording and evidence. Future parallel-event counting mechanics must define what is being counted before grading.

## 9. Accessibility audit

- Every four-witness comparison has a linear text alternative: `witness → accuser → claim → Peter response → evidence → confidence`.
- Ordering tasks support keyboard move-up/move-down or numbered placement; no required drag-and-drop.
- `TX1` is announced as text before grading and never encoded only by icon/color.
- Retrieval prompts have no speed requirement; screen-reader users are not penalized for navigation time.
- Optional evidence is explicitly announced as optional and never blocks mission completion.
- Feedback announces node result, source reason, confidence class and mastery consequence in that order.
- Long passages are navigable by headings and verse groups; focus returns to the task prompt after source inspection.
- Emotional language about Peter is never converted into judgment about the player’s faith, character or holiness.

## 10. Source-audit verdict

`SOURCE_AUDITED` for all answer-bearing claims in required nodes against the assigned Gospel passages and the existing canonical TX1 policy. The mission deliberately leaves exact cross-Gospel accuser identity and full microchronology as `D1` rather than forcing a single reconstruction.

## 11. Completion conditions

`MISSION_COMPLETE` when:
- N01–N14 are completed through independent or guided routes;
- the prediction is retrieved before fulfilment evidence is used;
- all four Gospel witnesses are represented in final synthesis;
- Mark `TX1` is handled without translation penalty;
- no final claim falsely universalizes witness-specific evidence;

`perfect-investigation` requires independent completion of N01, N03, N07, N11, N13 and N14 plus at least one optional node; it increases knowledge/mastery evidence only and carries no spiritual-status meaning.
